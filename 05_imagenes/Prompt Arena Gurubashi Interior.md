# Prompt — Interior de la Arena con la Unidad de Karga

> Momento de la campaña: se muestra a los jugadores la unidad de [[Karga Furiadiestra]] campeando el interior de la arena durante [[La Prueba del Cénit]]. El resultado debe pegarse en esta carpeta (05_imagenes) con nombre claro y referenciarse en [[El Foso de las Mareas]].

## Prompt

```
Cinematic interior view of an open-air gladiatorial arena built into a rocky crater on a jungle island, painterly splash-art style of World of Warcraft concept art. Wide packed-dirt ring, sun-bleached and dust-filled, ringed by reinforced wooden palisades and scattered trophies and spoils. Camped inside the ring, staring at the entrance from the shade of the palisade, a motley elite unit of adventurer-aspirants: a large imposing female orc warrior standing with arms crossed, the clear leader and the most threatening; a bulky tauren shaman leaning on a huge bone maul, totems at his belt; a sharp-toothed troll hunter in a feathered hat with a colorful winged serpent coiled around his shoulder; a tiny gnome witch in a stained coat waving a finger mid-argument while a gnome rogue with a thin silver dagger smirks at her, the two mid-squabble over a chunk of meat on the ground between them; apart from the group, a tall, utterly still night elf warrior sitting on a stone block with a long two-handed greatsword resting vertically between her knees. Warm late-afternoon light, dust motes, tense watchful calm before action, highly detailed, cinematic composition.
```

## Puntos clave que no pueden faltar

- Los 5 miembros + Karga de pie como cabecilla.
- El tauren con su mazo de hueso, el trol con la serpiente alada, la gnoma y el gnomo discutiendo por la carne, y la elfa sentada y silenciosa con la espada.
- La arena: tierra apisonada, palizas, trofeos variados alrededor.
