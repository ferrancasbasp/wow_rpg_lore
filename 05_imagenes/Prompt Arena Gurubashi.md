# Prompt — Entrada de la Arena Gurubashi

> Momento de la campaña: los jugadores se acercan por primera vez a la Arena durante [[La Prueba del Cénit]], bajo el bloqueo de [[Karga Furiadiestra]]. El resultado debe pegarse en esta carpeta (05_imagenes) con nombre claro y referenciarse en [[El Foso de las Mareas]].

## Prompt

```
Cinematic wide shot of the main entrance to an open-air gladiatorial arena on a remote jungle island, painterly splash-art style of World of Warcraft concept art. The arena is built inside a wide rocky crater pit: a packed-dirt fighting ring below, sun-bleached almost white, ringed by wooden palisades reinforced with rusted iron studs and spikes, torn banners with muted faction colors hanging limp. In the foreground, a huge double gate of reinforced timber stands closed under a threatening heavy lintel. Guarding it, a handful of rugged adventurer-aspirants: a large imposing female orc warrior in battle-worn gear, plus two or three mixed-race fighters, lounging with studied boredom, hands resting near weapons, eyes watching. Jungle treeline rim the crater's top, warm golden afternoon light, dust motes in the air, long shadows, tense vigil atmosphere, highly detailed, cinematic composition.
```

## Puntos clave que no pueden faltar

- La doble empalizada de la entrada, grande y con púas.
- La arena en el cráter, apisonada y blanquecina.
- La unidad de Karga vigilando (orca imponente y sus aspirantes), aburridos pero a punto.
