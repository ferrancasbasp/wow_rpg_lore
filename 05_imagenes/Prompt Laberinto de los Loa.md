# Prompt — Entrada del Laberinto de los Loa

> Momento de la campaña: los jugadores descubren la entrada del templo en la jungla durante [[La Prueba del Cénit]]. El resultado debe pegarse en esta carpeta (05_imagenes) con nombre claro y referenciarse en [[El Laberinto de los Loa]].

## Prompt

```
Cinematic wide shot of the entrance to an ancient half-buried troll temple swallowed by thick jungle, painterly splash-art style of World of Warcraft concept art. A weathered grey stone façade rises among creeping roots and moss; two broken columns flank a doorless dark entrance. Across the lintel, a worn bas-relief interweaves three Loa symbols: a crouching panther, a bat with spread wings, and a coiled serpent. Lines of carved runes run along the façade like dried ink catching the last light, faintly ominous. The ground before the threshold is strangely bare of leaves, as if swept every night. Dense twisting jungle all around, shafts of sunlight breaking through the canopy, drifting mist, mystical and tense atmosphere, painterly, highly detailed, cinematic.
```

## Puntos clave que no pueden faltar

- Las runas Loa talladas sobre la entrada.
- Los tres símbolos en el dintel: pantera, murciélago y serpiente.
- El frontón devorado por la jungla, dos columnas rotas.
- La entrada sin puerta, oscura.
