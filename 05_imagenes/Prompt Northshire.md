A small humble stone cathedral with warm golden windows stands at the center, its heavy wooden doors just opened; an elderly priest in simple robes stands at the threshold observing a small band of eight young adventurers who are gathered on the cobblestone steps, adjusting their gear and speaking quietly amongst themselves.
