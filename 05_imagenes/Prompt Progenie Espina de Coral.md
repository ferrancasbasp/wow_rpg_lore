# Prompt — Mercado de la Progenie Espina de Coral

> Momento de la campaña: los jugadores entran en la cueva-mercado de las pozas costeras durante [[La Prueba del Cénit]]. El resultado debe pegarse en esta carpeta (05_imagenes) con nombre claro y referenciarse en [[La Progenie Espina de Coral]].

## Prompt

```
Atmospheric interior view of a vast sea-cave turned into an improvised market, painterly splash-art style of World of Warcraft concept art. Shafts of daylight fall through cracks in the high rock ceiling and reflect off still pools of deep green water. Serpentine naga merchants coil around coral platforms and beached shipwreck timbers, trading among improvised stalls made of coral, driftwood and dried kelp: stacks of polished scales, weapons forged of hardened coral and carved bone, casks of sealed fresh water, woven mats heaped with dried goods and clamshell baskets, and curious trinkets of unclear origin. Bioluminescent algae and dim crystals tint the scene in teal and violet; water gently laps at wet stone, faint mist, warm highlights. The naga trade with slow serpentine movements and flicking fin gestures. Highly detailed, cinematic, mysterious but calm commerce mood.
```

## Puntos clave que no pueden faltar

- Interior de la cueva con luz cayendo en haces.
- Puestos improvisados de coral, madera varada y algas.
- Nagas comerciando (efecto serpenteante, gestos con aletas).
- Mercancías: armas de coral, escamas, barriles, cosas de origen dudoso.
